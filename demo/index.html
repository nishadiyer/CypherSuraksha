🛡️ AI Shield — live demo

This page plays a still picture as if it were a participant on a video call, so you can watch AI Shield's pill react. It is a demo only: real calls are live video.

Needs the AI Shield demo build loaded in Chrome (npm run build:demo, load dist-demo/). The pill appears on the video within about 10–20 seconds. Sample pictures come from public research datasets and are not part of the repository.
